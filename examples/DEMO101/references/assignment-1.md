# DEMO101 Assignment 1

Worth 10% of the final grade. Submit one PDF.

A café records the daily high temperature (x, in °C above 20) and the number of iced drinks sold (y, in tens) over five days:

| Day | x | y |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 | 2 |
| 2 | 2 | 4 |
| 3 | 3 | 5 |
| 4 | 4 | 4 |
| 5 | 5 | 5 |

**Question 1 (20 marks).** Compute the least squares estimates b₀ and b₁ by hand. Show Sxy and Sxx.

**Question 2 (15 marks).** Interpret the slope and the intercept in the context of the café. Is the intercept meaningful here?

**Question 3 (25 marks).** Compute the five residuals, SSE, SST and R². State in one sentence what R² tells the café owner.

**Question 4 (25 marks).** Compute the standard error of the slope and a 95% confidence interval for β₁. Based on the interval, is there evidence of a linear relationship?

**Question 5 (15 marks).** Sketch the residuals against the fitted values. Comment on whether the linearity and constant variance assumptions look reasonable.
